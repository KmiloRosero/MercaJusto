# Surface brief: MercaJusto mobile app

Scope: all screens of the Expo app in `mobile/` (buyer, producer, courier). Visitor mode: **Operate**.
Audience/job: urban buyer in Pasto completing a purchase with confidence; campesino producer publishing and selling. Constraints: outdoor daylight use, low-end Android phones, low digital literacy, weak signal. Material 3 controls and navigation stay standard.
Build path: code-led (Operate surface, no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: MercaJusto looks like the Nariño hillsides it sells from: the "colcha de retazos" patchwork of fields. It refuses the delivery-app default of electric blue, orange promo badges and the same grey product square for everything.

OWN-WORLD: Field green #1F6B3D carries actions, the app bar of the brand moments, and selection; clay #A8501C (dark: #F2A56E) is reserved for excedente/discount; a green-tinted neutral ground #F7F8F3 with white cards (dark: green-black #0F1410 with tonal surfaces). Every category owns a field colour (ochre tierra, leaf green, mora red, maize yellow, páramo sky, coffee brown) shown as tile ground + ink. All text pairs ≥4.5:1, tokens as Material roles (onPrimary, containers).

STORY: The buyer sees fresh produce from named fields, finds the action in one green, and spots excedentes by their clay colour.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: greeting + location, search field, the green excedente banner, a carousel of surplus products whose tiles carry their category's field colour, chips, grid. Primary actions in field green; tab bar active state in green.

FORM: Pinned by the user ("Verde campo nariñense"); concept-seed not run because a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Seed key: user-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
